### API gotchas for this DSL, worth folding into the skill

- `border_width=0` is rejected ("must be greater than 0"). To fake borderless, set `border_color` equal to `fill`.
- **Shape text defaults to `align=left`** even though the DSL spec documents centre as the default. Every shape needs an explicit `align=center` or the design system's centring rule is silently broken. 46 shapes on YT 01 had to be corrected after the fact.
- Process titles at `size=47` overflow the 695px box past roughly 20 characters. Use `size=36` to `40` for longer titles.
- `DOC` and `TABLE` treat x/y as **top-left**, not centre, unlike every other item type.
- Board-level items (no `parent=`) have no bounds constraint, so they cannot fail the batch on geometry. Items with `parent=` can, and a violation fails the entire batch.
