## 2 · What the X export says

90 days, 26 May to 22 Aug, 377 rows. Split by content input rather than by impressions, per the standing rule.

| Input | Count | Impressions | Median impressions | New follows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Original posts | 120 | 5,767 | 30 | 3 |
| Replies | 257 | 10,338 | 17 | 8 |
| **Total** | **377** | **16,105** | | **11** |

Also across the full 90 days: 173 profile visits, 5 bookmarks, **0 URL clicks**.

**The week-over-week jump is real.**

| Week | Originals | Replies | Impressions | Follows | Profile visits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10-16 Aug | 23 | 30 | 2,505 | 1 | 27 |
| 17-22 Aug | 26 | 201 | 10,442 | 7 | 111 |

Reply volume went up 6.7x. Impressions went up 4.2x. Profile visits went up 4.1x. Follows went from 1 to 7.

**The honest read on that.** Reach and profile visits scaled almost exactly in line with reply volume, which means the replies are landing in front of people rather than dying. 38 of the 201 got a reply back, so roughly one in five started a conversation. Only one reply in 201 got zero impressions.

What did not move: bookmarks (2 for the week), URL clicks (0 for the entire 90 days), and anything downstream of a follow. Seven follows is the output of 227 pieces of content in six days.

**This repeats the finding from the 20-21 Aug measurement session, on Mauro's own account.** Volume correlates with reach. Reach is not yet correlated with anything that pays. The difference is that on a 90-day-old account with 16k total impressions, building reach is the correct thing to be doing, so the volume is not wasted. It is just not evidence of anything yet.

**Two caveats, stated so they are not skipped.**

1. The export cannot tell me who wrote the replies. The voice reads as Mauro's and the reply-voice skill was being tuned in the same 72 hours, which is consistent with the reply engine running, but attribution is unverified.
2. Four days is not a trend. The comparison week also contains a 44-day posting gap earlier in the series, so the baseline is soft.

**What Juan deserves credit for, objectively.** The volume is up 6.7x, the quality bar held (one in five replies drew a response, and the highest-reach reply of the entire 90 days landed on 19 Aug), and it ran without Mauro checking it. That is the delegation working. The thing it has not yet done is produce a single measurable outcome past a follow, and nothing in the current setup would catch it if it did.
