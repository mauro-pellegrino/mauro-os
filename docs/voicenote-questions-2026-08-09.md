# Voicenote questions, 9 Aug 2026

**Why these:** `brand/operating-baseline.md` says the offer is the blocker and posting is second. `docs/NEXT-STEPS.md` has 10 YouTube calibration questions still open. These pull the raw material out of your head so those get closed. Nothing here re-asks what `business-context-answers.md` already answers (ICP, beliefs, writing style, goal, hours).

**How to record:** one voicenote per question. Say the number out loud first. Two to five minutes each, rambling is fine and better than tidy. Don't prepare, don't edit. Send them in any order.

**If you only do six:** A1, A3, A8, B2, C4, C7.

---

## A. The offer (the actual blocker)

**A1.** Walk me through the engine you run at the agency, start to finish, like you're explaining it to another agency owner on a call. What happens Monday, what happens by Friday, what comes out the other end, who touches what.

**A2.** The $28k deal off X. The whole story. What they saw, what the first DM said, what happened on the call, why they bought, how long it took.

**A3.** An agency owner hands you $10k tomorrow and gives you 30 days. What exactly do you install? List the actual artifacts they have in their hands at the end of it.

**A4.** Go back through that list. Which parts genuinely need you, and which parts could Juan or a Claude skill do without you in the room?

**A5.** Name three real agency owners you could message this week about it. Who they are, what you know about their situation, what would make each one say yes.

**A6.** What's the part of this you don't want to sell? What question are you scared they ask you on that call?

**A7.** Price. Say out loud the number that feels uncomfortable to charge, then say what you think it's actually worth to an agency doing $300k a month. Don't reconcile them, just say both and explain each.

**A8.** What would you have to see in the first 30 days to know this offer works? And what would make you kill it instead of tweaking it?

## B. X growth

**B2 is the one that unlocks the most.** Everything in your feed for the next 90 days can come out of it.

**B1.** Read your best-performing post out loud and tell me why you think it landed. Then the one that flopped hardest, same treatment.

**B2.** What did you build, fix, or figure out at the agency in the last seven days that you'd never think of as content? Describe it in full detail, including the boring parts and the thing that went wrong.

**B3.** Name ten accounts you want to be seen by. For each, what would you have to post for them to actually reply.

**B4.** What do you believe about AI content that would annoy other agency owners? Say the harshest version, then say why you're right.

**B5.** Talk me through yesterday honestly. What did your hour on this brand actually go to.

**B6.** What's something you know how to do that you assume every agency owner already knows how to do?

**B7.** Think of a conversation where you explained something and the person said "wait, say that again." What was it, who was it, what were their exact words back to you.

**B8.** You have to post five days a week for 90 days and you're not allowed to do any new work. What are you posting, purely from what already exists in the repo and your head?

## C. YouTube

You said you're lost, so most of these are not asking you to already have a strategy. C6 to C9 are the four decisions only you can make, and they're short.

**C1.** Five videos you've watched all the way through in the last six months, in or near your lane. Why you clicked, and why you stayed past two minutes.

**C2.** The video you already published. What you liked about making it, what you hated, what you'd never do again.

**C3.** Describe your ideal filming day honestly. On camera, screen sharing a real build, or slides with voiceover. Pick the one you'd genuinely still be doing in month six.

**C4.** What's the video you wish existed when you started building this stuff, that nobody has made? Describe it like you're pitching it.

**C5.** Someone binges your channel for two hours. What can they do at the end that they couldn't at the start?

**C6.** Series or one-offs. The repo contradicts itself here (`docs/work-log-apr-aug-2026.md` says every idea must be an episode of a repeatable series, a later verbal call reversed it to one-offs with Charlie Morgan style titles). Say which one you meant and why, so I can correct the loser.

**C7.** Channel identity. Is it Mauro the person or ghostedcalls the brand? And what's the job of a video: subscribers, feeding X, or booking calls. Pick one primary.

**C8.** Cadence and length. What can you honestly ship every month for six months without it dying. Eight minutes tight or twenty minutes deep.

**C9.** Which numbers are cleared to say on camera. The ~$300k/mo agency, the third from organic, the $28k deal, anything else. Every public number needs your sign-off.

**C10.** Name four to six channels you want me watching for you. They fill the `[MONITORED CHANNELS]` placeholder in the research and YouTube skills.
