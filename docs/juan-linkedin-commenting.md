## How to comment

**Timing**

1. **Comment before we post.** In the 30 to 60 minutes before our own post goes live, leave 5 to 10
   comments. People who click through from a comment land on a fresh post.
2. **Be early on their post.** The first hour of their post is when most people read the comments.
   Turn on the bell for the 20 profiles so the notification arrives.
3. **Daily volume:** 10 to 15 comments, about 30 minutes. Never more than 1 comment a day on the
   same person.

**What a good comment looks like**

- **2 to 4 lines.** Long enough to say something, short enough to read in the feed.
- **Add one specific thing from our real work:** a number, a format we ran, a step in our system.
  Example shape: "We saw the same with [format]. [What happened, with the real number]. The part
  that surprised us was [detail]."
- **Agree and extend, or add a second angle.** A respectful "one thing we see differently" is fine
  when it is true.
- **Write like Mauro talks.** Plain words, no buzzwords. Read `brand/voice.md` first.

**Never**

- "Great post", "So true", "Thanks for sharing", or an emoji alone
- A pitch, a link, or "DM me"
- A comment written by AI and pasted without edits. People recognise it, and so do the creators
- A number we cannot prove. If the specific is missing, write a question in its place

**After**

- Reply to every reply on our comment, the same day.
- Accept every connection request that comes from these threads.
- Log each day in one line: date, profile, post topic, replies received, profile visits that week.
  After 2 weeks we keep the profiles that send visits and swap the rest.
