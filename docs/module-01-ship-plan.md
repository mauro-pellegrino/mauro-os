Three reasons to finish 01 first anyway.

**One. The product has already changed shape three times in eight days.** Cashie offer filed 17 Aug, $500 self-serve filed 18 Aug, restructured as an eight-module engine 21 Aug. A fourth pivot on day nine is the pattern, not a correction to it. Nothing has ever been sold in any of the three shapes, so there is no evidence any shape was wrong.

There is not enough magnet distribution on Mauro's own account to conclude anything about magnets. What the data shows is that the account is small, which is a different problem.

**Three. Module 01 is 80% done and module 02 is 0%.** Switching converts a nearly finished asset into two unfinished ones.

**What to do with the article examples instead.** Collect them now, build later. They are the single hardest input to reconstruct and they should not sit in a folder on the Mac. Drop them in `research/x-articles/` with a one-line note per example on why it worked. That is 30 minutes and it makes module 02 a packaging job when its turn comes.

Revisit the order the moment either of these is true: module 01 has a paying buyer, or the four validation scripts come back with people asking for articles instead.
