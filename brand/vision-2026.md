## The clock

Today is 13 Aug 2026. The stated goal is $5k/mo from this brand by end of 2026.

That leaves **20 weeks**. At the 5 to 7 hours a week Mauro says he'll protect, the entire remaining budget is **100 to 140 hours**. For a buyer running a $300k/mo agency, $5k/mo is one or two clients. Not an audience.

Everything below is sized against 120 hours, not against an open-ended runway.

---

## 1. The actual enemy

Named on 1 July, in his own words: *"I hide in building systems because it feels productive, instead of shipping my own thing and putting it in front of people. I optimize what I can measure and avoid the uncomfortable work of selling."*

Six weeks of git history since then supports it. 46 commits from Mauro plus 17 from Juan, almost all of them skills, research and infrastructure. Two people producing steadily. In the same period: no offer defined, no X analytics baseline pulled, no post published, no outreach conversation logged.

Capacity is not the constraint, and the merge proved it. Building produces a visible artifact every session. Selling produces rejection. That asymmetry is the enemy.

---

## 2. Anti-vision (what's at stake)

It is 31 December 2026. The brand has made $0. The X account is still around 200 followers. There is still one YouTube video.

The repo has grown to twice its size. There are skills for lead magnets, articles, long-forms, YouTube boards, Miro design systems and DM setting, all of them well built, none of them ever run against a paying client of his own. The 26 voicenote questions are still unanswered. Juan kept producing all year and every batch landed in the repo instead of in front of a buyer. `[BOOKING LINK]` and `[MONITORED CHANNELS]` are still placeholders.

The agency is fine. It always was. That's what makes this version survivable, and that's why it's the likely one.

The version he wrote himself, and which the evidence points at: he keeps riding the agency's channels, and the thing that was supposed to be his own stays a hobby that gets his leftover hours.

*This is the evidence-based anti-vision. The personal run may produce a sharper one. If it does, that one wins.*

---

## 3. Vision (how you win)

Running the agency and his own brand at the same time, earning from both, with one or two agency-owner clients paying for the install of the system he already runs daily.

The install is the thing he uniquely can sell: he architected and runs the AI content and acquisition engine behind a ~$300k/mo agency, at least a third of that revenue comes from the organic accounts he manages, and he closed a $28k deal off X.

Identity statement to test against the personal run:

> I am the type of person who sells the system he already runs, instead of building another one.

---

## 4. The mission (end of 2026)

**$5k/mo recurring from one or two agency-owner clients.**

Break: one client at $5k/mo, or two at $2.5k. Both are well inside what his own proof supports. `operating-baseline.md` already flags the $100 workshop idea as the wrong price for this buyer.

**The one concrete thing that proves the pattern broke:** a signed client who found him through his own channel, not the agency's.

---

## 5. The boss fight (next 30 days, to 12 Sep)

**Define, price and sell the offer.**

Not build anything new. Everything needed already exists in the repo.

| Week | What ships |
|---|---|
| 1 | Section A of the voicenote questions answered. Offer doc drafted from them: promise, deliverables, price, delivery process |
| 2 | X analytics exported, first `ops/acquisition/` baseline built, Monday analysis run for real |
| 3 | Three named agency owners contacted directly about the offer |
| 4 | First call held. Offer revised from what actually got asked on it |

The gate at day 30 is one sales conversation held, not one asset shipped.

---

## 6. Daily levers (the quests)

The split from `operating-baseline.md`, costed against 6 hours a week:

| Lever | Share | Hours/week |
|---|---|---|
| Repurpose real agency work into X posts and proof | 60% | 3.5 |
| Direct outreach and DMs to agency owners about the offer | 25% | 1.5 |
| One monthly YouTube build | 15% | 1.0 |

Content is packaging of work already done. It is not net-new creation. Anything that reads as net-new creation is the enemy in disguise.

**Juan is producing, and the output is not the constraint.** 17 commits merged on 14 Aug: the JK Molina reference library, two new skills, voice corrections, a short-form batch and a YouTube batch. What's missing is the merge and review loop, so his work reaches main the same week instead of sitting on a branch. Two things still need Mauro: the Phase 1.1 folders that remain, and picking 4-6 monitored channels so `[MONITORED CHANNELS]` resolves.

---

## 7. Constraints (the rules)

1. **5 to 7 hours a week.** The agency comes first and pays for everything. This is real scarcity, not a soft target.
2. **No new skills until there is a client**, unless a live client engagement requires one.
3. Nothing publishes without Mauro's review.
4. Never name clients in public-facing content.
5. Never fabricate a number. Bracket it or leave it out.
6. No Growthub material in this repo, ever.
7. Articles and lead magnets never get saved to the repo.
