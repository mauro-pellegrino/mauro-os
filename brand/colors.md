# Brand colours

**Set 20 Aug 2026.** Cream base plus the palette pulled from the Drake embassy render: warm lit stone, deep chocolate paving, amber step lights, near-black.

| Role | Hex | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | `#4A392C` | Notion page covers, section headers, the dark block on cover cards |
| **Base / cream** | `#F5EFE4` | Page backgrounds, doc grounds, the off-white in doodles |
| **Accent** | `#E0A854` | Highlights, CTAs, callouts, the one thing on a page that glows |
| **Ink** | `#171310` | Body text, borders, near-black fills |
| **Stone** | `#D8C9B4` | Secondary fills, table headers, muted panels |
| **Grey** | `#8C8880` | Captions, metadata, disabled states |

## Rules

**One primary, everywhere.** Every Notion magnet cover is a solid `#4A392C` block. Reused on every asset so the pages read as one system rather than a pile of separate documents.

**Amber is the only saturated colour on a page.** It marks the single most important thing and nothing else. If two things are amber, neither reads as important.

**Cream is the ground, not a highlight.** It replaces plain white in docs, HTML infographics and doodle backgrounds.

**Where this came from.** The reference is a warm dusk render: lit limestone, deep brown paving, gold step lights against near-black. Warm and expensive rather than bright and clean. That is the register.

## Existing conventions this replaces

- Article doodles keep their own muted `#F3E3A3` on off-white, which sits inside this family already
