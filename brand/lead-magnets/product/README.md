## What the first Notion assembly got wrong

Recorded so the rebuild doesn't repeat it.

**No examples.** Page 6 exists as a full worked magnet in the repo and never made it in. A buyer who can't see what done looks like builds the wrong thing.

**No templates.** The post structure, the DM, the LeadShark config, the cover spec and the page structure are all written in `_master.md` and none of them shipped. A couple of hook lines went in instead.

**Prompts without the system behind them.** Prompts alone are the weakest form of this. They're the fallback for someone stuck in a chat window, not the product.

**No install path.** Nothing told the buyer how to actually get the system running, which is the only thing they care about on day one.

---

## Three rules for the rebuild

**1. Assume they've never heard of Claude Code.** Page 1 leads with the path that needs no terminal. Claude Code is offered second for the ones who already have it.

**2. Every page carries the real artifact, not a description of it.** If a page mentions a template, the template is on that page in full, copy-pasteable. No summaries, no "the structure is roughly."

**3. The buyer's brand context is a required input, not an optional extra.** Page 2 exists because the same prompts produce noticeably worse output without it. This is what replaces every "ask Mauro" in the internal skill files.

## Who this is for

Agency owners and operators who already produce good video but aren't converting it into written content. They have the raw material and no format. They want to run it themselves rather than hand it over.

Concretely: the Camilo Castañeda / Brando / Antonio Ventre profile. Real operators with a real body of work, comfortable with AI tools, not developers.

**What that means for the writing.** Peer to peer, no hand-holding on marketing fundamentals, no explaining what a lead magnet is. They know. What they don't have is the mechanics, the templates and the reasoning behind each decision.
