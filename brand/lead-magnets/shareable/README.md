## What was stripped, and why

These are not the internal files. Removed:

- Internal file paths (`accounts/...`, `research/...`) that mean nothing outside the repo
- Internal tooling: LeadShark config, the UTM registry, the Client Acquisition staging page
- Team names and internal correction history ("Mauro rejected two rounds on 07-31")
- Anything client-identifying

Kept: every rule, the formula, the $100 bar, the real analytics table, the seven rejected-to-shipped titles, the fatigue curve. The evidence is what makes it credible, so it stays.

## Pasting into Miro

One code widget per section reads better than one giant block. Split on the `##` headings. Set `data-language="markdown"`.
