## Finding 3: 33 original posts, zero follows, one bookmark

2,933 impressions across 33 originals. Nobody followed. One bookmark, and it was on a media post.

The text originals are well-built and they fail the same way. 21 of them are 40 to 56 words stating a principle: "Volume without a map is guessing with a bigger budget" (53 imp), "Judge every post against your product and your price" (72 imp), "does line one make them stop" (57 imp). Median 67 to 76 impressions, ceiling 179.

Nobody bookmarks a principle. They bookmark a process they can run. The substance is there and the shape is wrong.

The two highest-reach originals were media with no text body, 332 and 201 impressions, against 179 for the best text post. Media (6 of 33 originals) out-reached text roughly 2x per post.

## Levers for next week, ranked

1. **Stop buying impressions in AI Twitter.** The top 10 replies by reach produced 3,858 impressions and zero follows. Those rooms are a vanity channel for this account. Redeploy that reply time to accounts whose replies are about agency acquisition, pipeline and client work.
2. **Pick 15 rooms and live in them.** 119 accounts got exactly one reply. Build a fixed list of 15 accounts whose audience is agency owners, reply daily under each, hold it for 3 weeks. Baseline to beat: @simonnyyberg's 4 replies to 2 follows.
3. **Cut the 11 dead accounts in Finding 5.** 39 replies a week returned zero. That budget moves to the list from lever 2 at no extra cost.
4. **Lead replies with what he runs, not what he thinks.** Every follow-driving reply named real work: booked calls, the engine he runs for the B2B agency, the weekly analysis. The zero-follow ones were opinions anyone could hold. One instruction, 35-fol-per-1k behind it.
5. **Change the shape of originals, not the volume.** 33 a week is fine. Convert the principle posts into save-shaped assets: numbered process, screenshot of the actual build, the Monday post-to-call match with real numbers. Measure on bookmarks, floor of 2 to beat.
6. **Ship 2 media posts on Tue and Thu.** Media out-reached text 2x per post on 6 posts, and the overview export shows zero media views on every day of the week. Use `skills/content/visual-docs/mauro-visual-doc-system.md`.

## Sample-size flags

- One week, no prior period. Nothing here is a trend yet.
- 2 bookmarks and 0 shares are too small for a rate. The direction is unambiguous regardless.
- Every per-account read except @simonnyyberg (4), @chanduuu_cs (6), @TTrimoreau (5) and @paolo_scales (4) rests on 3 or fewer replies. Directional.
- The script's "impressions by length" table shows the 0-15 word bucket at 201 median, but all 3 posts in it are media posts whose text is only a t.co link. That is a media effect, not a length effect. Length is flat from 15 to 60 words (74 and 67 median). Do not shorten posts on the strength of that table.
- Content export totals (12,360 imp, 9 follows) differ from the overview (13,041 imp, 29 follows). X attributes only some follows to specific posts. Use the overview for totals and the content export for per-post reads.
