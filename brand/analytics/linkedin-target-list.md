## The qualification rule

These thresholds are read off how the CSV was already sorted. [CALIBRATE: Mauro to confirm the 10k and 60k edges, they are the two calls that move the most accounts.]

1. **Followers between 10,000 and 60,000.** Under 10k the comment section is too small to be worth a daily rep. Over 60k the comment section is crowded and the audience has gone general, so a comment gets buried.
2. **Posted in the last 24 hours.** A fresh post is the only one worth commenting on.
3. **At least 3 posts in the last 7 days.** Monthly posters give nothing to show up to.
4. **Lane green:** B2B acquisition, LinkedIn or content growth, personal brand, ghostwriting, outbound and cold email, AI systems applied to a service business.
5. **Their audience is his ICP:** agency owners, B2B founders, consultants. This is the one that actually decides it. The person is the door, their commenters are the room.

**Lane red, skip regardless of size:** ecom and Shopify, Meta or Google ad creative, performance media buying, brand video production, corporate L&D. That is the agency's service, not Mauro's lane (`CLAUDE.md` section 1).

## The lookalike test (for anyone not on the list)

Mauro asked for the people who **are the same as these**. Run the five qualification rules above, in order, and stop at the first failure. Then one more read before adding them:

- **Check the last ten posts, not the one that happened to fit.** One on-lane post does not make a green room.
- **Read their comment section, not their follower count.** If the commenters are agency owners, operators and B2B founders asking real questions, it is a green room. If it is "great post!" from other creators and job seekers, it is an engagement pod and the room is worthless no matter the number.
- **Altitude.** Anything pitched at first client, first $1k, quitting the 9 to 5, or "how I got 1000 followers" is a beginner room. Skip it, same rule as X.
- **Brand safety.** Fraud, slurs, doxxing, compliance dodging, crude framing. A comment puts his name in that screenshot permanently.

**Where the next batch comes from.** This one came out of the comment sections of Paolo's and Eugenio's posts. Keep mining the same way: take a post from someone already on TARGET, open its comment section, and run every commenter above 10k followers through the rules. The people commenting on acquisition posts are selecting themselves.
