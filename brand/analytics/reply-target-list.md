## How to maintain this

1. Every Monday, Mauro exports X Analytics > Content for the last 7 days into `research/x-analytics/`.
2. Run `python3 tools/x-reply-targets.py --since <last Monday> --csv <that file> --rows`.
3. Any account that produces a follow moves to KEEP.
4. Any account with 3+ replies, 0 follows and 0-1 profile visits moves to DROP.
5. Any TEST, BIG ROOM or BUYER account still at 0 follows and 0-1 visits after 5 replies moves to DROP.
6. Any account whose posts are about models, tools or benchmarks moves to RED, no matter the reach.
7. Any account that posts at beginner altitude ($1k MRR, first client, first $10k) moves to RED, no matter the reach.
8. The team (@lorenzo_pravata, @Bogzabs96) is never on the list.
9. The script prints "replies to roster handles". The target is 15 of 15 a day on the list.
10. When an account moves tier, update `docs/juan-x-list-handles.txt` the same day. Juan adds or removes the account on the X List "Reply rooms" by hand (no API access).
