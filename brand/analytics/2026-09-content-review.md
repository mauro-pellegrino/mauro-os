**Headline:** 818 posts, 89 originals and 729 replies. 59,716 impressions, 57 bookmarks,
442 profile visits. One reply carried 22% of all reply reach and it said "that's great, bro."
Mean impressions per original held flat all month (269 → 302 → 274 → 255) while originals per
week fell from 29 to 17.

## Two corrections to the 28 Sep read

1. **The reach peaks were not content.** Sep 4-5 spiked because of one-line banter replies under
   large accounts, not because of anything he published. The top reply of the month did 7,856
   impressions, 6 profile visits and 0 follows.
2. **Bare links are not the top bookmark format.** Across the month short originals took 26 of 57
   bookmarks against 21 for bare links. The Sep 9-15 window that suggested otherwise was one week.

  three of the four top profile-visit posts are all short originals.
- **Replies are the profile-visit engine and nothing else.** 70% of profile visits, 2 bookmarks
  across 729 posts, median 14 impressions.

anything on the per-post follow column. Profile visits is the working outcome column.

## Levers for October

1. **Restore original volume.** Mean impressions per original held at ~270 all month while the
   count fell 29 → 17. At 29 originals a week that is roughly 3,200 additional impressions per
   week with no change to the writing.
2. **Build the asset the Sep 5 post promised.** 2,315 impressions and 16 bookmarks for a
   description of the weekly analysis. It is the clearest demand signal on the account and there
   is nothing to send the people who saved it.
3. **Raise bare-link frequency.** Median 532 impressions against 156 for everything else, and only
   14 of 89 originals. The floor is the point, they do not need to go viral.
4. **Reallocate replies.** 236 of 729 replies went to 31 accounts. @wizofecom returned 214 imp per
   reply over 6, @OnatAksaray 9.6 over 20, @jeffbullas 7.2 over 5. Median reply is 14 impressions.
5. **Post more capture and x-mechanics content.** 14 posts between them, the two highest
   profile-visit rates on the account.
