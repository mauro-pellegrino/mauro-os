# Your Brand Context

**Fill this in before you build anything.** Fifteen minutes. It's the single biggest difference between output that sounds like you and output that sounds like a tool.

Paste the finished version into your Claude Project, your folder, or the top of every chat. The system reads it before it writes a word.

**Why this page exists.** The same prompts produce noticeably worse magnets without it. Not slightly worse. The difference between something you'd put your name on and something you'd quietly delete.

---

## 1 · Who you serve

One paragraph. Specific enough that someone outside it would rule themselves out.

Not "ecommerce brands." Something like "founders doing $200k to $1M a month on Meta who've plateaued because their creative testing has no system behind it."


**The test:** would a reader recognise themselves in one sentence, or do they have to squint?

## 3 · Your proof

Real numbers only. Whatever you'd say out loud on a call and stand behind.

```

```

**Hard rule the system enforces:** it will never invent a number. If this section is empty, it writes without numbers rather than making them up. That's the right behaviour and it produces weaker copy, so fill it in.

## 5 · The live problem

What is unresolved in your niche **this month**. Not this year.

```

```

**Why this is dated.** The title formula needs something concrete and currently urgent, and urgency expires. This is the field you come back and change. Everything else on this page is stable for a year.
