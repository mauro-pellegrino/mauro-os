# The Magnet Scoring Sheet

Score a magnet before you ship it. 20 checks, four sections. Fill it in, add up the score, read the verdict.

This exists because the system's own rule is that a magnet must be a thing you fill in rather than a thing you read. A rulebook with nothing to fill in fails its own test. This is the page that fixes that.

**How to use it:** one point per box you can honestly tick. Half marks don't exist. If you have to argue for a tick, it's a zero.

---

## Magnet being scored

```
Title:
Subtype:            prompt swipe file / framework / case study / video / industry
Pages:
Date:
```

---

## Section 1 · The title (5 points)

| ✓ | Check | Why it fails without this |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | It names an **artifact noun** (guide, playbook, checklist, sheet, kit, system, workflow, prompt file, stack, template, swipe file, breakdown, formats, prompts) | The reader can't picture what lands in their DMs |
| ☐ | It carries a **number or a year** | Nothing signals current or finite |
| ☐ | The topic is broad enough that a beginner recognises it | Reach dies at the first word |
| ☐ | The thing named is **concrete and currently unresolved** in your niche | Broad topics get views. Concrete urgent ones get claimed |
| ☐ | Zero invented concept nouns, zero questions, zero diagnoses | "Why Your Ads Stopped Scaling" promises nothing received |

**The one-line test:** could you hold up the title and have someone tell you what object they'd receive? If no, rewrite it.

**Score: ___ / 5**

---

## Section 2 · The $100 bar (6 points)

| ✓ | Check | Why it fails without this |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | There is **at least one page they fill in**. A blank field, a row to score, a template to complete | This is the whole bar. Without it you shipped an article |
| ☐ | That fill-in page is **second to last**, not buried in the middle | The last two pages are where the value sits. The final page is the CTA |
| ☐ | It contains at least one of: paste-ready prompts with slots, a real swipe file, a scoring artifact, or a filled-in worked example | Those four are the only things that clear the bar |
| ☐ | If it ships prompts: **at least one tells them no** | A prompt chain that only ever says yes isn't a system, it's a wish |
| ☐ | Every example is **real**, not fabricated | One invented dashboard and the reader who checks is gone permanently |
| ☐ | Someone who read it would **do something differently tomorrow** | The final test. If not, the writing quality is irrelevant |

**Score: ___ / 6**

---

## Section 3 · Structure and visuals (5 points)

| ✓ | Check | Why it fails without this |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Every section is a **real nested child page**, not a heading or toggle on one long page | A 7-page magnet arriving as one scroll reads as a blog post |
| ☐ | Page count is **5 to 7** | Under 5 feels thin. Over 8 and they quit before the fill-in page |
| ☐ | Every image **adds** something. Cover the page text: does the image still teach? | An image that restates the text is decoration |
| ☐ | No infographic or "key takeaways" graphic that repeats the page it sits on | Explicitly banned |
| ☐ | Anything with a table, a checklist, aligned boxes, or more than ~30 exact words was **rendered as HTML and screenshotted**, not generated | Image models get text wrong and you can't edit it later |

**Score: ___ / 5**

---

## Section 4 · Cover, post and delivery (4 points)

| ✓ | Check | Why it fails without this |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | The cover is a **clean screenshot of the asset's own first screen**, cropped tight, no browser chrome, title legible at thumbnail size | A designed card promises. A screenshot proves |
| ☐ | The post's bullets each name a **deliverable**, not a topic | "The split you're aiming for" beats "how to think about your funnel" |
| ☐ | The DM **names the page to start on** | That single instruction is the difference between a download and a read |
| ☐ | You know which **vehicle** this magnet posts as, and it hasn't run in the last week | Running one shape repeatedly is what kills the mechanism, not the volume |

**Score: ___ / 4**

## Verdict

**Total: ___ / 20**

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **18-20** | Ship it. |
| **15-17** | Ship it, then fix what you missed before the next one. |
| **11-14** | Don't ship. One section is broken. Find the section scoring lowest and rebuild that part only. |
| **0-10** | This is a document with a magnet's title. Go back to Section 2 and build the fill-in page first, then re-score. |

**Any zero in Section 2 is an automatic hold regardless of total.** That section is the bar. Everything else is polish on top of it.

---

## The three failures that account for most low scores

**A clever title.** Every rewrite in the correction log went the same direction: stop being clever, name the object.

**No fill-in page.** The rules are handed over in full, the reader nods, nothing changes. The most common way to score 14 and feel like you scored 19.

**An image that restates the page.** Easy to produce, adds nothing, and it's the fastest signal to a sharp reader that the asset was padded.
