# The Engine

**Page 1 of everything. Read this before any module.**

## What this is

I run a content and acquisition engine for a B2B agency. Every week it turns work that already happened into content, and content into booked calls. It's measured against calls, not impressions, and it takes a few hours a week to run.

This is that engine, broken into modules you can install one at a time.

You're holding **module 01**. It's complete on its own. It's also one part of something larger, and knowing the shape of the larger thing is what stops you building a lead magnet that has nowhere to go.

---

## The five stages

Every piece of content in the engine moves through the same five stages. Nothing is invented at any point.

**1 · Source.** The raw material. Client calls, a build you shipped, a video you recorded, a problem you solved on Tuesday. This already exists. You are not creating it, you are noticing it.

**2 · Extraction.** AI turns the source into usable pieces. Beats, verbatim lines, angles, the one claim worth leading with. This is where most of the leverage sits and where most people skip straight past.

**3 · Formats.** The pieces become things people receive. Lead magnets, articles, posts, infographics, docs. One source, many formats, and the format decides the shape before a word is written.

**4 · Distribution.** Where it goes and how often. The channel, the cadence, the auto-DM that turns a comment into a conversation.

**5 · Measurement.** Which content produced a conversation. Not likes. Conversations, and then calls. This is the stage that tells you what to do more of, and it's the one almost nobody builds.

## What module 01 gives you

By the end of it you will have:

- A repeatable way to turn any video or transcript into an asset people ask for by name
- The rules behind every decision, with the numbers that produced them
- Every prompt written out, paste-ready
- One complete magnet built start to finish so you can see what done looks like
- A scoring sheet to run before you ship, not after
- The post, the DM and the auto-DM config that deliver it

Roughly fifteen minutes per magnet once you've built one. The first one takes a few hours.

## How to run this

**Install one module. Use it for a month. Then take the next one.**

The failure mode with anything like this is reading all of it and installing none of it. The modules are deliberately separable so you never have to hold more than one in your head.

If you install module 01 and never touch another, you have a working lead magnet system and that was worth doing on its own.

---

## What this is not

**Not a course.** There are no lessons and nothing to complete. It's the actual system, handed over, with the reasoning attached so you can change it when your situation differs from mine.

**Not theory.** Every rule in here exists because something got shipped, measured, and in several cases rewritten. Where a number appears it's real. Where I don't have a number I say so.

**Not finished.** The engine gets corrected most weeks. That's why the files live in a repo rather than a frozen copy: when I fix something, you get the fix.
