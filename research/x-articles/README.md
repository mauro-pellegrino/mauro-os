## What this folder is for

**@maurojpelle's own published articles, logged with their outcomes.** That is the one thing the growthub corpus cannot hold, because it is other people's articles and their bookings are invisible.

Every article this account publishes gets a row in `~/growthub-os/research/article-studies/PREDICTION-LEDGER.md`, with:

- its declared intent, `reach` or `convert`
- what the skills predicted before it went out
- impressions at 7 and 30 days
- its unique DM keyword and the DM count
- bookings in the 3-day window

**A prediction written after the number is known proves nothing.** Fill the prediction columns before publishing.

## Why this matters more than more captures

The reach rules are settled enough to use, drawn from 41 captures across 24 accounts. **The convert rules are not.** They come from twelve `@lorenzo_pravata` articles against a different ICP: brand operators spending $100k+/mo on Meta, where this account sells to agency owners installing AI systems.

Until this account's own articles are logged, the convert lane here is running on a borrowed hypothesis.
