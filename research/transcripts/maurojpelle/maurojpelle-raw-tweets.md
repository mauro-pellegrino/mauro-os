## The voice.md workflow (contrarian how-to) — 2026-09-04

Instead of using prompts for your content + setting money on fire, do this;

1. Record yourself speaking for 10min
2. Create a voice.md
3. Save inspo content from bigger accounts
4. Connect winning formats with the voice.md

Making sure you sound like yourself is the most important part.

It's how I manage to create banger content for all of the accounts I manage.

Each one gets a different style depending on their speaking, not writing.

**Why it works as voice:** contrarian instructional opener ("instead of X + setting money on fire, do this"), numbered steps as the payload, then a flat confidence line grounded in his real operation ("all of the accounts I manage"), then a specific insight that only he could say from doing the work ("depending on their speaking, not writing"). Ends on the insight, no CTA. The "speaking, not writing" line is the ownable non-native-speaker / record-your-voice angle from voice.md, stated plainly.
