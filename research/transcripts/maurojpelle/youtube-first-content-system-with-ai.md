## Key beats (for repurposing)

1. **YouTube-first because it trickles down.** Focus all content effort on one high-quality YouTube video, and every other platform (LinkedIn, X, Instagram) gets easier and better because it's derived from that one source.
2. **YouTube builds trust faster for high-ticket.** A 12-minute video of you speaking on a topic you know builds more trust than one LinkedIn or X post, and trust is what closes $10k+ agency deals.
3. **The platform migration.** Started on X, moved to LinkedIn ~18 months ago, now going hard on YouTube, especially with AI.
4. **AI is why the decision works now.** Building skills in Claude Code (YouTube MCP + skills saved natively) makes the whole system fast: titles, scripts, competitor research.
5. **The skill-iteration framework.** Build a skill (e.g. scriptwriting), test it on real scripts, keep iterating it and asking Claude to edit it. Don't be complacent, the first version is usually bad and even a "good" one has room to improve.
6. **The target output.** 2 to 3 high-quality YouTube videos a week, mapped out as a full AI content system.
7. **The goal.** A fast content machine that produces a lot of leads. Stated goal: a million a month.
