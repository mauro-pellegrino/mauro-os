## Extracted answers

**The window.** 90 days to a call, self-serve. The thing being built is a distribution system.
This reconciles with the paid offer rather than contradicting it: `research/jk-molina/cashie-studio/offer/agency-booked-calls.md`
promises 2 booked calls in 30 days done-with-you, with Mauro in the loop. 90 days is what
the reader should expect running it alone off an article.

**Day-zero activities, before any content exists.** Comments on very targeted profiles, and DMs.
Both available from day zero, both help growth on their own. Extra leverage if a YouTube channel
already exists.

**Content is not just the posts.** "You do content that is great, and you also do the things that
come with content that make your content better." The surrounding activity (comments, replies, DMs)
is part of the system, not overhead.

**What the reader owns at the end.** The exact tasks, plus skill files or agents they can install in
Claude Code or whatever AI they use, and the weekly input tracking run the way the agency runs it.

**The angle (the "why this and not the crowded thing" section).** Everyone is doing AI content. The
move is AI content that does not read as AI. Use AI to sound more natural and more original, not
less. Mauro's own method is the proof: he speaks in voice notes and the voice is what the writing is
built from.

**What a subagent is for, and what it is not.** A subagent is good at collecting ideas and raw notes.
The human effort goes into displaying them the way the algorithm rewards. X articles are working
well right now, and the failure mode is pasting a YouTube video into an article and using the
YouTube thumbnail as the cover.

**The roadmap, in Mauro's order.**

1. **Build the brain.** A company brain or voice document. Every call transcription, all client work,
   all case studies, all wins, the wins channel, everything.
2. **Pick one channel.** X first if X is the channel. Do not run X and LinkedIn at the same time.
3. **Choose and track the inputs, weekly.** Comments, replies, DMs sent, articles published per week.
4. **Find competitors doing what you want to do.**
5. **Split the output by vehicle.** Giveaways, articles, short-form tweets, each as its own lane.

**Audience.** Agency owners, confirmed.
