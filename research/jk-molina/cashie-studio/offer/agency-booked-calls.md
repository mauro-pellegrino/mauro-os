## The offer in one line

Get an agency owner **2 booked calls in 30 days** using a lead magnet system built on Claude.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Agency owner of a creative agency, or an operator running Meta/ecom ads |
| **Promise** | 2 qualified booked calls in 30 days |
| **Price** | $200/week for 4 weeks ($800 total) |
| **Guarantee** | No booked call in 30 days → free work until they get at least 1 |
| **Spots** | 1 |
| **Closes** | 30 July 2026 (expired) |
| **Framing** | Founding intake. Explicitly a pressure-test, buyer knows they're first |

## The three components

1. **The Booked Call Blueprint** — weekly 1-on-1 call mapping their situation, positioning, and fastest path to a booked call
2. **The Lead Magnet Engine** — the internal lead magnet structure rebuilt for their offer, with the Claude prompts and templates
3. **The DM Sequence Stack** — the outreach sequences that turn content consumers into booked calls

## Delivery

- One 1-hour 1-on-1 call per week
- Slack access between calls
- A place where templates and "what's working internally" get shared
- He shows them how. He does not do it for them.

## Formalised, 17 Aug 2026

Mauro's decisions on the open issues.

**Deadline: dropped.** Standing offer, not a dated launch. Scarcity is the 1 spot.

**IP: his.** No clearance needed. All agency naming stripped from the repo and from this copy.

**The ICP, sharpened.** This is the real change. The buyer is not "any established agency owner." It is:

- Already produces **good YouTube videos**. The raw material exists.
- X and LinkedIn are either **solid or dormant**. Either way the video isn't being converted.
- What they lack is the **formats**: lead magnets, X articles, HTML docs. The things the Claude system produces.
- They want to **set it up themselves** rather than have it run for them.

Named examples of this exact profile: **Brando**, **@antonioventre**.

That last point changes the delivery obligation. If they're setting it up themselves, the teaching has to be **precise**, not directional. A weekly call plus Slack access does not cover it on its own. The templates and the walkthrough have to be good enough to follow without Mauro in the room.

**Delivery idea: a Skool channel.** Somewhere the templates, walkthroughs and "what's working" live, rather than dripping them through DMs. Not decided, worth pricing out. It also answers the distribution question Cashie deferred and never returned to.

**What this makes the qualifier.** "Do you already have YouTube videos?" A yes means the input exists and the system has something to run on. A no means they're not ready, and that is a clean disqualification rather than a maybe.
