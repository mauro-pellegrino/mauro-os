## The offer in one line

Get an agency owner **2 booked calls in 30 days** using a lead magnet system built on Claude.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Agency owner of a creative agency, or an operator running Meta/ecom ads |
| **Promise** | 2 qualified booked calls in 30 days |
| **Price** | $200/week for 4 weeks ($800 total) |
| **Guarantee** | No booked call in 30 days → free work until they get at least 1 |
| **Spots** | 1 |
| **Closes** | 30 July 2026 (expired) |
| **Framing** | Founding intake. Explicitly a pressure-test, buyer knows they're first |

## The three components

1. **The Booked Call Blueprint** — weekly 1-on-1 call mapping their situation, positioning, and fastest path to a booked call
2. **The Lead Magnet Engine** — the internal lead magnet structure rebuilt for their offer, with the Claude prompts and templates
3. **The DM Sequence Stack** — the outreach sequences that turn content consumers into booked calls

## Delivery

- One 1-hour 1-on-1 call per week
- Slack access between calls
- A place where templates and "what's working internally" get shared
- He shows them how. He does not do it for them.

## Open issues before this ships

Flagged by me on filing, not by Cashie.

**1. The close date has passed.** 30 July, 18 days ago. Re-date before any of the validation scripts go out, or the scarcity reads as fake on the first line.

**2. It sells Growthub's internal IP.** Two lines commit to handing over "every template and Claude prompt we use internally at Growthub" and DM sequences "tested inside a $300k/month agency." That is Lorenzo's material and Lorenzo's number. This needs his explicit sign-off before it goes to anyone, and it is a separate conversation from the one about Brando.

**3. The deliverables don't exist yet.** Mauro said it in the chat: the templatised Claude skills are still to be built, and the "which templates work" library needs creating. Cashie handled this correctly by framing the whole thing as a founding intake, which is honest. Worth knowing the founding framing is load-bearing, not decoration.

**4. It does not reach the stated revenue goal, and shouldn't.** $200/week at 1 spot is $800/month. `vision-2026.md` targets $5k/mo. This is a validation offer whose job is a first paying client and a first case study. The revenue vehicle comes after. Keep the two separate rather than judging this one on the $5k number.

**5. The ICP is narrower than the repo's.** `audience.md` says established agency owners at mid six figures a month and up, generic. This offer says creative agencies and ecom ads operators who want ecom brands as clients. That is a sharpening, and it may be the better positioning. It needs reconciling either way.

**6. A prior attempt didn't land.** In the chat: "I didn't get them any calls sadly @antonioventre." Relevant to the guarantee, which promises free work until at least one call happens.
