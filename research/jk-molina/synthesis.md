## The whole library is one ladder

**Followers → Leads → Customers → Core Clients → Top Clients**

| Stage | How they arrive | Mechanism doc |
|---|---|---|
| Followers | Whale Bait (extract YOU: Ideas, Philosophy, Games, Updates) | `whale-bait`, `the-whale-brand` |
| Leads | Insight-Based Content (extract TRUTH: Stories, Outcomes, Problems, Insights) | `value-vs-insight`, `the-20-minute-email` |
| Customers | A $50-100 one-time offer | `the-customer-funnel`, `1000-new-whales` |
| Core Clients | 55 people at $250/week | `the-offer-shell`, `minimalist-plan-part-1` |
| Top Clients | 12 people at $500/week | `the-offer-shell`, `pricing` |

67 people plus some customer offers = $84k/month = $1M/year. His framing: "if you had great offers and you made them daily for 1 year, could you get 67 people and some change to say yes?"

## The eight mechanisms

**1. Value vs Insight.** Value shows what to do. Insight shows where to look. The gate is one question: *has this person given me more than $0?* No → insight only. Yes → insight plus value. His evidence: two value-based videos produced 0 clients, one insight video produced 5.

Frame for writing insight: **What → Why → Where → Bridge Gap.** The What is a Problem, an Outcome, or an Idea. The bridge differs by channel (socials ask for a reply or a follow, email asks for a booking or a buy).

**2. Offer Shell vs Offer Suite.** A suite is separate products where improving one doesn't help the others. A shell is one offer (access to you) sold at rising tiers, so every improvement bleeds into every tier.

| Tier | Delivery adds | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Assets | the stuff itself | $10-300 |
| Temporary Access | community, group calls | 2-10x assets |
| Group Access | 1:1 onboarding | $100-400/week |
| Long-Term Deals | perks | varies |
| 1:1 Access | DMs, 1:1 calls | 2-3x group |
| Partnership | hands, IP | 10% of profits |

Revenue bands: Assets plus Temporary plus Group ≈ $10k/mo. Add Long-Term and 1:1 ≈ $100k/mo. Add Partnership ≈ $100k/mo+.

**3. Prospect Inertia.** A customer is worth ~50 leads. Getting $100 first makes someone 56x more likely to buy next. 80% of his clients come from a workshop. 25% of workshop buyers became clients. Workshop customers are 0.1% of his audience and 80% of his income.

The missing step most people skip: **Leads → Customers → Clients.** Going straight from lead to a $1k+ client offer is a jump most won't make.

**4. Whale Bait vs Fish Bait.** Fish bait is "get your first client online", "top 10 mindset books", "here's my entire playbook for free". Four whale bait buckets: **Protagonism** (a declared journey people tune into), **Philosophy** (what you refuse to do), **Problems** (explaining why, never solving), **Proof** (what already happened).

Ten fish-bait traps he names: thinking about people who never paid you, focusing on peers over prospects, boxy language ("coaching, program, mentorship"), saying yes to too many things, calling your avatar out ("if you're a 6-7 figure entrepreneur"), defensiveness, shaming, niching down too hard, asking followers what they want, making content you don't enjoy.

**5. Audience Respect.** Three legs, ~3-4 months to build. **A Game To Tune Into** (declare a specific number, then post progress updates). **A Leader To Believe In** (insight posts: Problems, Outcomes, Ideas). **A Process To Stick To** (Real Scarcity, Real Urgency, Real Consequences).

Real scarcity comes from capacity: raise prices at 25%, 50%, 75% capacity. Cap intake per month. Cycles run 1-2 weeks, never evergreen, always with a closing date.

**6. Sawdust.** Don't create content, collect it. The table is the result, the sawdust is the work. He can only talk about the $100k month once, and his best-performing emails are about the sawdust. 50 extraction prompts included in `the-20-minute-email`.

The 20 Minute Email checklist: is it about 1 thing, have I cut what doesn't serve that thing, is it insight not value, am I honest, what's the natural next step.

**7. Packaging: Tool + Promise.** The Tool is a proprietary name (capitalized, like a tool in a toolbox). The Promise is what they get, and it must pass the **Meaning Test**: show it to two people and it means the same thing to both.

| Fails | Passes |
|---|---|
| Transform your physique | See your six pack |
| Create your Personal Monopoly | Make $10,000 |
| Become a master of productivity | Finish your workday by noon |

**Magic Pill:** a wrapper that makes the thing look easy. "Waitlist" sold 9 copies. "The 90 Days Of Offers Swipe File" sold 117.

**Big Idea test:** you have one when someone other than you uses it in a sentence.

**8. The Cash Script.** Five stages, no maybes: **Scouting → Position → Offer → Follow Up → Yes / No / Waitlist.**

Position means offering your doc as the answer rather than solving the problem in the thread. Follow-ups are consequence-based reminders, never persuasion. His conversion: 0.8% of email receivers reply, 21% of repliers say they're in, 63% of those actually pay. No CRM, just Gmail labels.

## The doc format itself

Consistent across all 16:

- Plain large title, no subtitle deck
- Opens on a belief or contrarian observation, one idea per line, heavy white space
- **Hand-drawn marker diagrams**: concentric circles, funnels, triangles, scribbled calendars. Never a polished infographic
- **Real screenshots as proof**: DMs, Stripe notifications, email threads, tweet analytics. Blurred, never recreated
- **Two-column comparison tables** as the main teaching device
- Numbers instead of adjectives
- Every framework named in proper-noun caps
- Closes on Recap → Action Steps / Implementation → Resources cross-linking the other docs
- 6 to 29 pages, most 15-22

## Where this collides with mauro-os

**The magnet philosophies are opposite.** `skills/lead-gen/lead-magnet/_master.md` requires $100 of work the reader didn't have to do, with paste-ready prompts and fill-in artifacts. JK requires the least value that proves you have the answer, because value attracts triers.

Resolved 14 Aug: keep both. The value model stays at `skills/lead-gen/lead-magnet/`. The insight model lives at `skills/lead-gen/lead-magnet-insight/`.

**the agency owner rejected the insight approach** for the agency in `#course-creator_club` (Oct 2024): "we sell to rich people that want to become richer, they want to save time and get there earlier, more than save a couple thousands." That argument transfers to agency owners at $300k/mo.

**What doesn't transfer.** JK sells coaching to solopreneurs, daily email offers, $250/week group access. Mauro sells an install to agency owners on 5-7 hours a week with no list, no landing page and no payment processor. The ladder transfers. The cadence does not.

**What it fixes.**

1. `operating-baseline.md` killed the $100 workshop as wrong for a $300k/mo buyer. JK's data says the workshop is the doorway that creates the client. The error was treating it as the whole offer.
2. "I don't have an offer" dissolves into the Offer Shell. Mauro's skills library is already the Assets tier.
3. $5k/mo at Core Client pricing is 4-5 clients, not 1-2.
