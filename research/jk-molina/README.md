## The 16 source PDFs

**Offer and pricing**
- `the-offer-shell.pdf` — one offer, tiered by access. The 6 tiers and their price bands
- `pricing.pdf` — 18 pricing plays. "Objective numbers, subjective feel"
- `servant.pdf` — the offer toolbox, why cycling beats one perfect offer

**Audience and content**
- `whale-bait.pdf` — whales vs fish, the 4 content buckets, 10 fish-bait traps
- `value-vs-insight.pdf` — the core distinction and the $0 gate
- `audience-respect.pdf` — a game to tune into, a leader to believe in, a process to stick to
- `the-whale-brand.pdf` — the 5 brand components
- `the-20-minute-email.pdf` — sawdust, collect don't create, 50 extraction prompts
- `likes-and-cash-workshop.pdf` — collect, desire, templatize, show don't tell

**Funnel and sales**
- `the-customer-funnel.pdf` — Leads → Customers → Clients, prospect inertia
- `1000-new-whales.pdf` — big ideas + cadence + variance, 6 big promos, 9 small promos
- `cash-script-2.pdf` — the 5-stage sell-by-chat script, inbound and outbound
- `scenario-swipe.pdf` — ~40 real conversation scripts by situation
- `the-100k-mo-email-list.pdf` — bundling and unbundling, offer cadence

**The plan**
- `minimalist-plan-to-1-million-part-1.pdf` — the model, 67 people to $1M
- `minimalist-plan-to-1-million-part-2.pdf` — 15 products and a 16-week calendar
