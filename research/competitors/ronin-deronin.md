## The Audience Radar concept (why this is dead-center Mauro's lane)

His system: scheduled Claude Code task listens to the ICP's real sources (subreddits, X accounts, YouTube comments), extracts pain points / repeated questions / objections / **exact phrases**, groups by theme and frequency, writes to context files, then feeds the content engine. Plus a competitor agent pointed at 5-10 competitors for gaps.

This is **belief #7 ("steal the customer's exact words") turned into software**, plus belief #12 (one source, many audiences) and belief #11 (volume with a map). Mauro already teaches this thesis; Ronin has packaged it into a nameable build. Strong candidate to rebuild in Mauro's voice for his ICP.
