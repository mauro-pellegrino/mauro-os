# AEO / LLM citation mechanics — Gamma's $100M ARR playbook

**Captured:** 2026-08-13
**Source:** *Distribution* podcast, episode 1. Hosts @subahwadhwani and @ZargaryanArthur, guest @ravishagrwal who leads Growth at Gamma. Gamma reached $100M ARR with AEO (answer engine optimisation) as their primary acquisition channel. Captured via a summary thread of the episode, author not recorded.
**Status:** every claim below is the guest's, unverified. Directional, not measured.

---

## Why this is worth a dig

The whole personal-brand play runs on X, and claim 17 below says nobody has ever seen X get cited by an LLM. If that holds, X still books calls but contributes nothing to the AI discovery layer that is quietly becoming how people find operators and tools. That makes owned pages and YouTube the only two citable assets, and it makes "what does ChatGPT say when an agency owner asks about X" a question worth actually testing.

---

## 1. How the models actually source answers

- **ChatGPT answers roughly 60% of queries from the live web and 40% from memory.** Half the game is being fetchable today, half is having been indexed already.
- **ChatGPT search runs on Bing.** Bing indexing is a prerequisite. Worth verifying independently.
- **ChatGPT memory compounds.** It re-recommends brands it has already shown a given user, so the first mover wins the citation and every repeat answer to that user afterwards.
- **ChatGPT does not appear to weight publisher authority.** A no-name blog gets cited about as readily as Forbes. The guest's framing is that this window closes as soon as the labs ship authority ranking, so the arbitrage is time-limited.
- **Claude barely cites anyone.** Writing content to get cited in Claude is close to wasted effort. Gamma's response was to stop trying and build an official Claude app instead, so "make me a deck" surfaces Gamma as a tool rather than as a reference.

## 2. What kind of page gets cited

- **Comparisons get cited, praise never does.** "Gamma vs PowerPoint" gets quoted in AI answers. "Why Gamma is great" does not. The model wants pages that adjudicate between options.
- **Your own site is a top source.** Gamma's #2 most-cited source is their own website, which means the comparison pages you write about competitors are substantially what the model repeats about those competitors.
- **Open content wins by default.** Medium and Quora still get indexed. Substack barely gets cited because writers gate their posts. Anything behind a signup wall is invisible.
- **Negative sentiment gets cited as readily as positive.** One negative Reddit comment stayed in ChatGPT's answers for months.

## 3. Platform-by-platform citation map

| Platform | Read by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Reddit** | ChatGPT only. Claude and Gemini ignore it. | Post to citation in 30 to 45 days. Small subreddits produce nothing, there is a size threshold. |
| **YouTube** | Yes, and view count is irrelevant | Cited from an 18k-view video the same as a 400k one. **Titles are what matter.** |
| **Medium / Quora** | Yes | Still indexed, open by default. |
| **Substack** | Barely | Gating kills it. |
| **X** | Nobody has ever seen X get cited by an LLM | The most consequential claim here. Verify before acting. |
| **LinkedIn** | Yes, chaotically | 500k-like posts and no-name posts cited at the same rate. No discernible ranking logic. |
| **Instagram** | Not yet, but showing up in search results | The guest's bet is IG and LinkedIn start shaping LLM answers within 6 to 12 months. |

## 4. Timing and decay

- **Broad topics take about 45 days to earn a citation, niche ones can take a day.** A Reddit post answering one very specific question appeared in Google's AI answers inside 24 hours.
- **A citation dies in roughly 3 months.** Gamma tracks every citation in a spreadsheet and re-wins the phrase quarterly. This is a maintenance channel, not a build-once channel.

## 5. Measurement

- **ChatGPT sends roughly 8x more customers than analytics shows.** Referrer data undercounts because people read an answer and then navigate directly or search the brand name.
- **The fix is self-reported attribution at signup.** "Where did you find us?" People answer ChatGPT even when click data shows nothing.

## 6. The weird frontier

- **Gamma optimises for AI agents, not just humans.** They write incentives like "10% extra if you sign up right now" into files only agents read, to influence what an agent picks on a user's behalf.
- **The guest's next bet: make the product something an AI can sign up for by itself.** Claude Code already installs several tools per session without asking anyone.
- **AI mode is collapsing click-through across the board**, which is exactly what makes citation-without-click the thing to optimise.
- **Copycat risk is real.** Clones copy the landing page and buy ads on the brand keyword, and users cannot tell which is real.

## What this suggests for the ghostedcalls play

Nothing committed, this is the shortlist worth testing.

1. **Comparison pages on the site, not positioning pages.** The citable version of a service page is "[approach A] vs [approach B]" for the decision an agency owner is actually making, not "why we're good".
2. **Add a "where did you find us?" question to the booking form.** Cheapest item here, and the only way LLM-sourced bookings ever become visible.
3. **Test one YouTube title written as a prompt** rather than as a title, and see whether it still earns the click. Prompt-shaped titles are what get cited, click-shaped titles are what get watched, and it is an open question whether one title can do both.
4. **Reddit is the untouched channel.** ChatGPT-only, needs a large subreddit, 30 to 45 days to citation. Answering very specific agency-operator questions is a plausible slow-burn play with a long half-life.
5. **X apparently contributes nothing to the AI discovery layer.** It still books calls, which is the measured outcome, so this is not a reason to stop. It is a reason not to let X be the only surface.
6. **Don't gate anything you also want cited.** A gated lead magnet and a citable article are two different jobs and need two different assets.

## Caveats

- Single source, one podcast, one company, self-reported. No independent verification of any percentage here.
- Gamma is PLG SaaS with a huge long tail of "make me a deck" queries. A high-ticket service business has a small buyer pool and thin query volume for anything like "best agency for X". The mechanics probably transfer, the volume almost certainly does not.
- The authority-ranking window closing is a prediction, not an observation. If it closes early, most of the arbitrage evaporates.
