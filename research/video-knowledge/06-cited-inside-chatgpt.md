## The one claim

There is an arbitrage window where a no-name page gets cited as readily as a big publisher, and the
window closes the moment the labs ship authority ranking.

---

## The information

### 1. How the models source answers

- ChatGPT answers roughly **60% of queries from the live web and 40% from memory**. Half the game
  is being fetchable today, half is having been indexed already.
- ChatGPT search runs on **Bing**, so Bing indexing is a prerequisite. The dig flags this as worth
  verifying independently.
- **Memory compounds.** It re-recommends brands it has already shown a given user, so the first
  citation wins every repeat answer to that user afterwards.
- **Publisher authority does not appear to be weighted.** A no-name blog gets cited about as
  readily as a major publication. This is the arbitrage, and it is time-limited.
- **Claude barely cites anyone.** Writing to get cited in Claude is close to wasted effort. Gamma's
  answer was to stop trying and ship an official Claude app instead, so the model surfaces them as
  a tool rather than as a reference.

### 2. What kind of page gets cited

- **Comparisons get cited. Praise never does.** "X vs Y" gets quoted in answers. "Why X is great"
  does not. The model wants pages that adjudicate between options.
- **Your own site is a top source.** Gamma's second most-cited source is their own website, which
  means the comparison pages you write about competitors are substantially what the model then
  repeats about those competitors.
- **Open content wins.** Medium and Quora are still indexed. Substack barely gets cited because
  writers gate posts. Anything behind a signup wall is invisible.
- **Negative sentiment gets cited as readily as positive.** One negative Reddit comment stayed in
  answers for months.

### 3. The platform map

| Platform | Read by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reddit | ChatGPT only, Claude and Gemini ignore it | 30 to 45 days post to citation. Small subreddits produce nothing, there is a size threshold. |
| YouTube | Yes, and view count is irrelevant | An 18k-view video is cited the same as a 400k one. **Titles are what matter.** |
| Medium / Quora | Yes | Open by default, still indexed. |
| Substack | Barely | Gating kills it. |
| X | Never observed being cited | The most consequential claim in the set. Verify before acting. |
| LinkedIn | Yes, chaotically | 500k-like posts and no-name posts cited at the same rate. |
| Instagram | Not yet, appearing in search results | The guest's bet is 6 to 12 months. |

### 4. Timing and decay

Broad topics take about **45 days** to earn a citation. Niche ones can take a day, and a Reddit post
answering one very specific question appeared in an AI answer inside 24 hours. **A citation dies in
roughly three months.** Gamma tracks every citation in a spreadsheet and re-wins the phrase
quarterly. This is a maintenance channel.

### 5. Why this matters specifically for a personal brand built on X

If the X claim holds, X still books calls and contributes nothing to the AI discovery layer. That
would make owned pages and YouTube the only two citable assets in the whole stack, and it makes
"what does a model actually say when an agency owner asks for a shortlist" a question worth testing
on camera rather than asserting.
