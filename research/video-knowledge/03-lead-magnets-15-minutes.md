## The one claim

The magnet is fifteen minutes of Claude work sitting inside a two-to-three hour cycle, and the DM
keyword is the only part of it that can be measured.

## The contradiction to handle on camera

The walkthrough says the full workflow takes about fifteen minutes with a VA covering the extra
parts. The SOP says an average lead magnet runs about two to three hours of hands-on work, then
distribution goes to the VA at around 6pm Argentina time.

Both are true and they measure different things. Fifteen minutes is the Claude pass that produces
the document. Two to three hours is ideation, format choice, the human pass, the cover, the post
copy, the DM copy and the handoff. Say both numbers out loud. Saying only the fifteen is the exact
overclaim the audience has learned to distrust.

## The information

### 1. The thirteen steps, grouped

**Phase 1, ideation and format, about 15 minutes.**
Ideation is the flagged bottleneck in the SOP itself: five to ten minutes, and explicitly worse on
paper than in Mauro's head. Triggers are a new week, a new YouTube video, a sales-call insight, a
trend, a competitor move, or a pattern from Monday tracking. Output is one topic and a one-line
angle. Then the format pick, three minutes: Notion doc with subpages (the five-pager, most common),
Gamma deck, an existing YouTube video used as the magnet, or Canva slides.

**Phase 2, production.** Context goes to Claude: topic, angle, format, and the topic-specific
inputs (source transcript, SOP, examples, brand context, audience, proof points). The SOP flags
that the skill should be asking for these rather than waiting to be fed them.

**Phase 3, packaging and distribution.** Cover image, X post copy plus DM message, LinkedIn post
copy plus LeadShark DM message, optional three follow-up images posted six to twelve hours later
with one-liners. The VA schedules at around 6pm Argentina time.

**Phase 4, Monday.** Tracking review of last week's winners, then iterate and exploit.

Cadence is deliberately not fixed. Magnets ship when ideation surfaces them.

### 2. The measurement, and why it is the whole point

From the article corpus: **the UTM Source field is filled on 4 of 541 bookings.** A unique DM word
per asset is the only attributable path from a piece of content to a booked call.
`[measured: growthub-os skills/content/x-articles/_corpus.md]`

That single line reframes the entire mechanic. The keyword is not a growth hack. It is the only
instrument on the dashboard.

### 3. The rule that stops the whole thing embarrassing you

A DM-trigger giveaway means the asset exists first. Never publish a post carrying a keyword or a
comment-to-DM CTA unless the resource it promises, and the thing that delivers it, are already
built and ready to send. The keyword is a promise. `[observed: standing rule in mauro-os CLAUDE.md]`

### 4. What the five subtypes are for

Prompt swipe file, framework, case study, YouTube video, industry-specific. The subtype is chosen
from what the topic actually is. A framework forced into a swipe file reads as padding, and a case
study forced into a framework loses the one thing that made it credible, which is the specific
account it happened in.
