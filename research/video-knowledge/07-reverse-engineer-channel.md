## The information

### 1. The method, and its honest limits

Web-only. The popular feed read through a reader service, thumbnails pulled from the image CDN and
analysed visually. **View counts are as-scraped and approximate.** The feed skews to the last one
to two years plus the one megahit, so a true all-time cut may surface older videos. Transcripts
were not obtained: YouTube and the free transcript sites blocked the fetch.
`[observed: caveats recorded at the top of the dig, 2026-07-16]`

Saying the limits out loud is the part that separates this from a guru teardown.

### 2. What the top ten looked like

One video at 638K, then a long drop to 52K, 43K, 39K and a cluster in the twenties and thirties.
The shape of that curve is itself the lesson: one outlier carries the channel, and the working
catalogue sits an order of magnitude below it.

### 3. The thumbnail formula

- **His face in nine of ten.** Serious, intense or somber. Almost never smiling.
- **Two archetypes, alternating.** Either a cinematic photo with one loaded prop that *is* the
  promise (cash stacks, an empty mansion, an EEG cap and a phone, a wall of tally marks, a tier
  list rail), or a hand-drawn whiteboard and a facecam for the framework videos.
- **Almost no typed text.** The image carries it and the title does the words. When text appears it
  is handwritten or part of the diagram.
- **High subject-to-background contrast**, moody dark or clean bright, filmic grade.
- **He reuses winners.** The brain-cap and phone motif appears at number one and again at number ten.

### 4. The title formula

Seven repeatable devices, all present more than once:

| Device | Example shape |
|---|---|
| Absolute plus curiosity gap | "You'll NEVER [x] again after watching this" |
| Fake-precise statistics | "99.6% of men", "50,000 men" |
| Time boxes and constraints | "Give me 13 mins", "12 hours a day", "10 years in 1 year" |
| Identity anchors | "millionaire CEO", "men" |
| Save-you-effort authority | "I ranked every [x] so you don't have to" |
| Hypothetical rebuild | "Step by step how I'd make [x] starting today" |
| Universal callout | "You all have the same problem" |

Topics are broad: money, discipline, dopamine, mindset. Nothing niche. That breadth is the
subscriber lever, and it is the thing to copy for a channel whose goal is subs.

### 5. The finding that validated the board system

His framework videos **are** the board format. A hand-drawn whiteboard or a live Miro board, a
winding road from a now state to a future state, a funnel, a named theory, narrated with a facecam
in the corner. Three of the top ten are exactly that.

So the split maps cleanly: the cinematic photo-and-prop thumbnail belongs on the broad hook video,
and the board itself is the visual for the framework video.

### 6. Named-framework packaging

"Time Compression Theory". "The Downfall Plan". "Now State to Future State". Every system gets a
proprietary name and a diagram. That naming is doing real work and it costs nothing to adopt.
