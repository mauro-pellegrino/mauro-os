## The argument, in his frame

Set a target you probably cannot hit and missing it takes you out. Set a realistic horizon and you
survive long enough for the thing to work. He calls the posture "negative" but corrects himself at
09:30: the honest word is realistic, or patient.

His words at 08:08: "vos tenés que ser negativo para ganar en contenido". The bad attitude is "my
first video got ten views, this is garbage". The good one is "I'm going to upload 50 youtube videos"
or "150 articles, and it'll start landing".

Why it matters commercially, 05:17: people who keep making content longest do best, and every
prospect who walks in wants their posts to hit now. This is the expectation he has to reset with
anyone who comes to work with him.
