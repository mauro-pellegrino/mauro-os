## The deeper findings these come from

**"How" is the strongest single predictor in the set.**

| Opener | n | median | outliers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| opens with "how" | 22 | 23,378 | 8 of 13 |
| everything else | 30 | 3,539 | 5 of 13 |

6.6x on the median. Not a hook style, a grammar. "how to [job]", "how a [thing] works", "how you
can [outcome]".

**Two grammars carry two engines.** Instructional posts are second person, "how to [you do X]".
Stack posts are first person, "my [domain] stack". The first-person ones are rarer and bigger. The
second-person ones are the daily engine.

**Everything is lowercase.** Every post in the set, titles included.

**A correction to the first pass.** I read the 189,705 post's repost-to-like ratio as evidence that
reference content is more shareable. Run across every item, the ratio is flat by topic, 0.049 to
0.083, with no separation. That was an n=1 read and it does not hold. Bookmark-to-like does
separate curiosity from reference; reposts do not.

## The candidates

| Vehicle | Stage | Platform | When to use | What makes it work | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch Translation** | TOF | X, LI | A platform or tool our ICP uses ships something | QT the announcement inside 24h. Title is "how to [job they already do] with [the new thing]". Numbered, carousel, no link. The launch carries the reach, we supply the framing it lacks | **adapt** |
| **How-To Carousel** | TOF/MOF | X, LI | The daily default | Opens with "how", numbered steps, carousel, no link in the post. This is the workhorse, 22 of 52 items and 8 of 13 outliers | **adopt** |
| **N-Layers Breakdown** | MOF | X, LI | Explaining the architecture of a system we run | "[thing] needs N layers", numbered, sub-bullets under each, carousel. Reads as a spec rather than advice | **adopt** |
| **Reference Piece** | MOF | X, LI | Converting arriving reach into follows | How to organise knowledge, context or process so it can actually be used. Highest bookmark-to-like ratios in the set, 1.5 to 2.4 | **adopt** |
| **Split CTA** | TOF | X, LI | Any time there is a link | The ask ships as its own post immediately after the main one. The main post carries no link. Expect roughly 700 views and never judge it on reach | **adopt** |
| **Account Study** | MOF | X, LI | Borrowing a bigger account's authority | Break one down by name ("I broke down @handle's system") or by number ("I read 52 posts from..."). The number form is stronger and is what a run of these produces | **adopt** |
| **Monthly Mega-Article** | MOF | X | Once a month | 12 sections, about 40k characters, images throughout. One artifact carried 11% of his month's reach | **adapt** |
| **Stack Reveal** | TOF | X | Showing what we run on | "my [domain] stack", numbered, one line per tool, every tool tagged. Carousel. Text can be one sentence | **reject for us** |

## Why Stack Reveal is rejected

It is the single highest-reach vehicle in the set, 35% of his reach from 10% of his items, and it
does not transfer.

It works because naming AI tools borrows the audience of every tool named. Our ICP's tools are ad
platforms and they do not amplify anyone. The mechanism is the tagging, not the list.

It becomes viable only if the stack we name is made of tools with their own audiences, which is a
different post about a different subject.

## Why Launch Translation is adapt rather than adopt

The mechanism transfers, the calendar does not. Our ICP does not follow model releases, they
follow platform changes.

Meta releases are our version of his tool launches, and our own data already agrees: the Andromeda
posts hit roughly 4x the comment rate of anything else on the account.

## Why Monthly Mega-Article is adapt

We already have the article system. What this adds is scale, 40k characters and 12 sections
against our current length band, and the observation that one artifact can carry a tenth of a
month.

Our own reach data puts the 1,800 to 3,000 word band at a median of 225,900 impressions. Whether
going five times longer holds that rate is untested here, n=1 on his account.

## What is deliberately absent

Video. Five videos in his set, zero outliers, 1% of reach. Nothing in the data supports building a
video vehicle off this study.
