For normal posts (not auto-DMs), write the way a real person actually types, not the AI-punchy style of super-short "trying to be interesting" sentences. The punchy style reads as AI and people can tell it's fake. Benchmark = a real story told in normal sentences, with a small honest admission, landing on a natural insight. **Why:** Accounts that perform look real and natural, like something a person actually wrote. What drives results is the only test that matters, and creative-to-stop-people staccato has not been it.

**How to apply:** Story-led, conversational, full sentences over staccato fragments. Stop forcing numbers-led punchy hooks on opinion/education posts. Two sharpenings Mauro flagged on the YouTube-first LinkedIn caption (2026-08-03): (1) the staccato ban is broader than three-word triplets, it also kills clipped short-sentence stacks and subject-dropped fragments strung together, especially as an opener ("We started on X. Moved to LinkedIn 18 months ago. Now everything's on YouTube." → fold into one flowing sentence, keep the subject); (2) "personality" does NOT mean adding invented voice. Mauro corrected this hard: personality means using HIS OWN words from the raw transcription, not invented metaphors/asides ("staring at a blank page", "it does the grunt work") and never off-voice words he'd never say (e.g. "scramble", which slipped in from a polished draft, not his real voice). When a line reads flat, the fix is to restore his actual raw phrasing, not to dress it up or flatten it into filler. The one edit allowed on his raw words is folding choppy stacking into flowing sentences. Also: simpler is better, do not over-work his words. EXCEPTION: auto-DM posts still use the punchy format (confirmed those have to be that way). This rule is for normal posts only. Casing: standard capitalization (sentence case) is the house style. Natural voice does NOT mean lowercase. ALL-CAPS only for the occasional emphasis word.
