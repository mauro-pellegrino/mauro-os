Mauro rarely leaves a short declarative sitting alone to restate or label a point. Lines like "That's the input." or "It's a repackaging job." read as written-for-effect, not how he talks. Fold the beat into the surrounding sentence with commas, even when that makes a longer, slightly run-on sentence. He talks that way.

This is not a ban on all short sentences. A single deliberate punch still lands. It targets the explanatory restatement specifically, the little label line that follows a point to re-say it.

**Why:** In a LinkedIn long-form draft, Mauro flagged "That's the input." and "It's a repackaging job." as lines he'd never say like that. The comma-folded version sounds like him.

**How to apply:** When a short line just re-labels the point before it, connect it with commas instead of a period.
