When Mauro pastes or links a tweet he's considering replying to, **lead with a straight reply-or-skip verdict as the answer**, not with drafted replies. The skip call is the value. Default to being the filter: if the post is thin/generic engagement-bait with low differentiation upside, say **skip** plainly and say why. Only draft replies when the verdict is a genuine "yes, worth it", or when he explicitly asks for the draft.

**Why:** Mauro grows through replies on the *right* posts. Making him reply to everything is engagement farming that wastes his time ("no me hagas contestar todo al pedo"). He wants me to protect his time, not manufacture reasons to reply.

**How to apply:** Run the [[x-reply-assistant]] Step 1 judge first and state the verdict up front. For a skip, don't offer draft options at all unless asked. If the call hinges on missing info (who posted it, how much traction), say so in one line and give a default lean rather than dumping drafts. Be honest per belief #13, a "your time's better spent elsewhere" is more useful than a yes on everything.
