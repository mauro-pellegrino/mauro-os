Never draft or ship a post with an autodm keyword or comment-to-DM CTA unless the resource it promises is already built and ready to send. The keyword is a promise to deliver. If the giveaway is a lead magnet, the skill that produces it and the finished asset both have to exist before the post goes out.

**Why:** Mauro's note on a lead-magnet long-form draft: "when including an autodm keyword comment you should probably have the skill prepared as well." A keyword CTA pointing at a resource that doesn't exist yet leaves people commenting with nothing to send them.

**How to apply:** When a draft includes a keyword CTA, prepare the deliverable alongside it, or flag clearly that it still needs building before publishing. Bracket the trigger word until Mauro confirms it (triggers need his sign-off). Codified in `brand/voice.md` sibling skills via CLAUDE.md section 7 Operating Rules and `skills/content/long-form/_master.md` CTA Patterns. Related: lead-magnet skills in [[feedback_lead_magnet_no_repo_save]].
