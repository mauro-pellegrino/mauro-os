# The Vehicle Library

**Pulled from Notion 20 Aug 2026.** 39 formats: 13 in active use, 26 from the wiz-of-ecom swipe library kept as reference.

**How to use it.** Pick a vehicle before writing anything. The vehicle decides the shape, the funnel position, and how much production the post needs. Reach for an **In use** vehicle first. The **Reference** ones are a swipe library to raid when nothing in the working set fits, not defaults.

Every entry: when to use it, what makes it work, funnel stage, and whether a voice note alone is enough to produce it.

---

## Voice note → LinkedIn: the runnable set

For Juan. These are the vehicles that need **nothing but a voice note**. No video, no dashboard screenshot, no design pass, no client data.

| Vehicle | Funnel | Why it works from a voice note |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority Long-Form** | MOF | Mechanism explained with real numbers. The voice note carries both |
| **Growth Long-Form** | TOF/MOF | One scaling insight, operator lens, contrarian pivot |
| **Personal Long-Form** | TOF/MOF | A personal story tied to the actual work. Must fit the process and reinforce positioning, never freeform opinion |
| **Story-Based Lessons** | TOF/MOF | A story carrying a business lesson, clear takeaway at the end |
| **Structured Business Take** | MOF | Bold statement challenging a conventional take, why the model is flawed, a structured fix |
| **Step-by-Step Breakdown** | TOF/MOF | Numbered steps, each with a specific action and a clear expectation |
| **Written Listicles** | TOF/MOF | A list post. Easy to consume, saves well |
| **Short Valuable/Relatable Take** | TOF/MOF | 3-5 short sentences on one overcomplicated belief, one actionable idea |
| **Hard Opinions on Business (RAW)** | TOF/MOF | Blunt, high-conviction, punchy structure |
| **Experience on Tough Times** | MOF | A failure or hard lesson. Builds trust through relatability |
| **Series Based Posts** | MOF | One subject over several posts, consistent opening, each standing alone |

**Eleven vehicles, zero production lift.** That's more than two months of a 5-day LinkedIn cadence before a single format repeats.

Everything else in the library needs an asset Juan doesn't have from a voice note: a video, a real dashboard, a designed graphic, or a named client result.

## Reference: the wiz-of-ecom swipe library (26)

A swipe library, not defaults. Raid it when the working set doesn't fit.

### Opinion and reaction

| Vehicle | Stage | When to use | What makes it work |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Live Industry Updates** | TOF/MOF | A big update happens in the space | Fast opinion-based reaction to a new trend. Short text or thread. Drives discussion |
| **Hard Opinions on Current Events** | TOF | A big shift or news hits the industry | Contrarian take on a trending topic, quick norm-challenging insights |
| **Hard Opinions on Business (RAW)** | TOF/MOF | Authority through bold, unfiltered perspectives | Blunt messaging, high-conviction narrative, punchy structure |
| **Opinions on Business / Trends** | TOF/MOF | Authority through bold perspectives | References current conversations, states an opinion, polarising angle |
| **Structured Business Take** | MOF | Present a rounded opinion or analysis | Bold statement challenging a conventional take, why the model is flawed, a structured fix |
| **Short Valuable/Relatable Take** | TOF/MOF | Engagement on a punchy tweet-inspired post | 3-5 short sentences, one overcomplicated belief, confident no-nonsense voice, one actionable idea |
| **Current Event Memes** | TOF | Engagement when a meme or trend is hot | Taps a meme, relates it back to business, strong hook |

### Teaching and structure

| Vehicle | Stage | When to use | What makes it work |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Step-by-Step Breakdown** | TOF/MOF | Explaining a complex concept | Numbered steps, each with a specific action, bullets or arrows, clear expectations |
| **Written Listicles** | TOF/MOF | Going viral with actionable content | Single tweet or thread as a list. Easy to consume, saves well |
| **Story-Based Lessons** | TOF/MOF | Teach through narrative | A story carrying a business lesson, clear takeaway at the end |
| **Series Based Posts** | MOF | Breaking a topic across multiple posts | Each builds on a subject, standalone value connected to a larger concept, consistent opening |
| **Threads with Videos or Examples** | MOF | Explaining a visual-heavy concept | Video breakdowns of strategies and campaigns, uses case studies |

### Proof and authority

| Vehicle | Stage | When to use | What makes it work |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Case Study Thread / Google Doc** | MOF | Proving authority through case studies | Thread or long-form step-by-step of a big win: problem, strategy, reasoning, solution, results |

| **Creative Showcase of Work** | MOF | Prove capability without hard selling | Creative presentation of real work, visual, implies authority |
| **Transformation** | TOF/MOF | Any before-and-after scenario | Structured before/after with a personal message or story |
| **Breaking Down an Expert** | TOF/MOF | Analyse a known expert, compound off their win | Visual carousel plus long form. Contextualise, highlight frameworks, list takeaways |
| **Stories of Other People** | TOF | A relevant person or brand is trending | Uses others' wins to tell a story, borrows authority, adds a takeaway |

## Notes

**Funnel coverage.** The library is heavily TOF/MOF. Only four vehicles are BOF, and three of those need real proof assets. That is the gap the wins log and social-proof folder would close.

**Source.** The 26 Reference vehicles come from the wiz-of-ecom Content Control Panel. They are a swipe library. Nothing here overrides `brand/voice.md`, and every vehicle still passes the pre-publish checklist before it ships.

**The live version** is the Content Vehicles database in Notion, related to the Content Calendar via the `Vehicle` property, so posts appear under each vehicle. This file is the repo-side snapshot.
