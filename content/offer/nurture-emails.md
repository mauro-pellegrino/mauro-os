## Email 1 · Idea · day 1

**Subject:** 185 tasks, 0 ticked

Thanks for grabbing the intake sheet. Here's something I found while building the system it comes from.

From 31 August I had Claude push my backlog into a task list every morning, 5 tasks a day. Last week I opened the files. 37 days, 185 tasks, 0 ticked.

I never went back to tick anything, because ticking is a separate job, and a separate job is the first thing I skip.

What did track my week was everything that writes itself: session logs, commits, the calendar Claude builds from them. So that's what my Monday reads now.

It's the same reason content systems die in agencies. If keeping it alive needs one more manual step from the owner, it lasts about as long as the owner's motivation, and the posts stop in week three.

The sheet is built on that idea. You paste numbers once and it does the scoring, the prompt and the record for you.

Mauro

## Email 2 · Problem · day 2

**Subject:** the video is already done

If you run an agency and you already make YouTube videos, you've done the hardest part of content. You sat down, you explained how you actually work, and it's recorded.

Then it goes up on YouTube and that's where it stops. The X account posts a link, or nothing. LinkedIn gets a clip once a month. Nobody turns the video into the formats that make someone raise a hand: a lead magnet, an X article, a doc they can use.

That gap is the whole reason I put together Agency Booked Calls. We aim to get you 2 booked calls in 30 days with a lead magnet system built on Claude, and it runs on the videos you already have.

One question decides if it fits: do you already make YouTube videos? If yes, reply and tell me your channel. If no, it's too early, and I'd rather tell you that now.

Tomorrow I'll send the details.

Mauro
