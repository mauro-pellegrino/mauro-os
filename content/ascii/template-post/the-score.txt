┌─ WHAT AN OUTLIER IS · measured against its own account ────────────────────┐
│                                                                            │
│   a post that did way better than that account normally does               │
│                                                                            │
├─ HOW WE SCORE IT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                            │
│   the number      impressions on x, comments on linkedin                   │
│   divided by      that account's median, last 10 to 20 posts               │
│   3x or more      an outlier                                               │
│   10x             a strong one                                             │
│                                                                            │
├─ THE FLOOR · so a tiny account doesn't win on ratio alone ─────────────────┤
│                                                                            │
│   on x            at least 50,000 impressions                              │
│   on linkedin     at least 300 comments                                    │
│                                                                            │
