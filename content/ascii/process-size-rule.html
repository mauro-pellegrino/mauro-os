==================================================================

  TOO NARROW                                        TOO BROAD
  "make the image"                          "linkedin strategy"
  nothing ships on its own                  no single output
  = a STEP inside something                 = a CHANNEL

|                                                                |

|   3  one person             who presses go                     |
|   4  one number             it moves on Monday                 |
|   5  between 6 and 12 steps                                    |
