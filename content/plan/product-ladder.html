adopt / adapt / reject

What fits 5 to 7 hours a week

Adopt

  • Free Claude Code skill as the magnet. AI Marketing Insiders gives 5. Your "skills" and "code" posts go up
  • Founding price that rises at a count. AI Operator: first 100. Camilo: 500
  • Close date. Cashie Studio, JK's 1 to 2 week cycles
  • Whop's 30% affiliate default, plus a higher rate for members
  • Personal welcome + "What's your focus?"

Adapt

  • Weekly drop comes from sawdust: work you already do
  • Live call: one a month
  • Drip unlock: one module a month, like AAS
  • Free tier: a free tool. The big rooms run on 995K YouTube subs or 947K emails
  • Promise: one scored account and one Gate pass in the first session

Reject

  • Comment-to-DM automation. X bans, Wiz call 28 Aug
  • Levels and badges. AAS: 48% stuck at Level 1
  • Daily calls, 24h support
  • Tool discount bundles. No partner deals
  • Skool. Whop picked 22 Aug
