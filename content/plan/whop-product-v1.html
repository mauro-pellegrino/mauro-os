the problem it fixes

A big number on a big account can be a normal day

You save posts because they did well. Then you copy one, and it does nothing. The post was never an outlier. It was an average day for a much bigger account.

The kit fixes the first step. Take an account's newest 10 to 20 posts, find the median, and divide each post by it. 3x is an outlier. 10x is strong. Under 50,000 impressions on X or 300 comments on LinkedIn, it does not become a template, whatever the ratio says.

> 5 Sep post: 2,316 impressions = 20x  STRONG

how it runs

One session, start to template

  1. Open a tracked profile. Start from your list, never from a random scroll.
  2. Score its newest posts. Each one against that account's median.
  3. Capture the outliers. Text, screenshot, link and the exact numbers, in one batch.
  4. Save the template. Claude reads the shape, checks for a duplicate and writes it to the store.
  5. Route and pick. The template goes to the process that runs it. You queue 3 to 5 for the week.
