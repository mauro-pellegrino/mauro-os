## 1. What the market looks like right now

### The short answer

- **Recurring is the norm for communities.** Every AI or content community found charges monthly or yearly: $19 to $129 a month, $199 to $2,999 a year. One-time is the norm for kits: $27 to $99.
- **The big ones sit behind a free tier.** AI Automation Society: 452,600+ free members, 3,700+ paid at $129/mo. Ruben Hassid: 947K+ free subscribers, 1K+ paid. The free room is the funnel.
- **The weekly drop is the retention hook.** "One advanced workflow in your inbox every week" (weeklyaiops), "weekly live build calls" (AI Operator, Build with Luke), three live sessions a week (Ruben).
- **Founding price is common.** AI Operator: $39 for the "First 100 members". Build with Luke: $19/mo founding. Camilo: $47 founding, $99 after 500 members.
- **Machina's paid community is no longer the model to copy.** weeklyaiops.com now says "It's free. Drop your email and get access right now." with "7,000+ users". The repo note (`research/competitors/machina-exm7777.md`) says "~$97/mo" with no source. That note is stale. The paid part is now an "inner circle" with no public price (machina.fyi).
- **One operator sells many small products from one audience.** Dickie Bush sold 15 products in October 2025, from a $27 ebook to a $6,800 program. "Only 4 existed at the beginning of this year."

systems only (`ownership-inventory.md`), no comment-to-DM (X automation bans, Wiz call 2026-08-28),
and the 22 Aug rule that recurring needs "modules arriving, weekly corrections and a live element"
(`ACTIONS.md` line 13).

| Mechanic | Seen at | Verdict | Reason |
