Every post format I run started as someone else's outlier.

The hard part is knowing which posts are actually worth copying, because a big number on a big account can just be a normal day for that account.

So I score every post against its own account. Take the newest 10 to 20 posts, find the median, and divide each post by it. 3x is an outlier, 10x is strong, and anything under 50k impressions on x or 300 comments on linkedin doesn't become a template, whatever the ratio says.

I ran my own account through it as the worked example. My best post of September did 20x my median and it still comes out under the floor, because a ratio on a small account only tells you about that account.

I turned the whole intake into a sheet you fill in:

1. Paste the posts, it gives you the median, the ratio and a verdict per post
2. A capture checklist for each outlier, the four things you send to Claude in one batch
3. The Claude prompt, written from your sheet, with the rule that it can't invent a reason the post worked
4. A template record you copy straight into your doc
5. A routing table and a weekly pick, so each template ends up in the process that runs it

It works for x and linkedin, and it saves in your browser.
