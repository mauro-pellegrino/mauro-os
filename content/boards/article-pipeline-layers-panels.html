Content, capture, convert

Every agency owner who has asked me why their content doesn't book calls has had the same shape underneath. Three layers, and only one of them shows up in a dashboard.

Content

Posts, articles, videos, the vehicles, the volume.

Visible: impressions, likes, followers
2

Capture

Something that takes an anonymous scroll and turns it into a name, an email and a phone number.

  • A lead magnet and a book-a-call page
  • A thank you page and a welcome flow
  • Emails going out every week
3

Convert

A CRM with real stages, follow-ups written by a person, someone getting on the phone.

Why it always presents as a content problem

Layer one is the only layer you can see. The twelve people who read your article, decided they wanted to talk to you, and found nothing to click are completely invisible.

The pipeline problem always presents as a content problem, so you keep treating the layer that is already working.

The inbound engine · convert layer

The part of my engine with no AI in it

I build AI systems all day. The convert layer is deliberately manual, because every lead's journey diverges and a sequence treats all of them identically, which is exactly why nobody answers a sequence.

@maurojpelle
What manual looks like

A board where the stages go past "booked a call":

Booked a call
Showed up
Said interested
Interested, not now
Contract sent
Went quiet

Three to five follow-ups a day, written by a human, each one referencing something specific from the actual conversation.

Where AI belongs
Run every lost call transcript, one row per lead
  • The objection they stated, quoted in their words
  • The objection underneath it, if the stated one was a proxy
  • What they said they were struggling with
  • The outcome they said they wanted
  • What would need to be true for them to buy
Now the follow-up answers the thing they said out loud.
For the ones worth it

Build them the strategy before they have paid you. A content plan plus three finished pieces, with the objection they raised answered inside it. They are talking to five agencies who all sound the same, and finished work is the only demonstration that survives that comparison.
