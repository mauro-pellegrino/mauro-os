Visual assets · the step before the style file

Author it, draw it, or capture it

Three ways to make a visual, and they are not interchangeable. The choice comes first, ahead of the style file, because picking wrong is where most of the time in this process goes and you only find out at the end, once the thing already looks finished.

@maurojpelle
author it
Named steps
A process, a comparison, a table, a grid, a matrix, a snippet shown exactly as written.
draw it
A metaphor
A feeling, a joke, an article header.
capture it
Evidence
A dashboard, a tracker, a real account, a real post.
Three ways to pick wrong
a table
asked of an image model, which gives you columns of confident nonsense in beautiful type, every word slightly off and none of it editable
a metaphor
authored as boxes and arrows, so you end up with a flowchart of a feeling that nobody wants to look at
a dashboard
redrawn clean because the real one has the wrong fonts and a sidebar, and the only part that was proof goes with it
No gate on this step
Nothing stops you choosing to draw something that should have been authored. The fix was ordering, putting the choice ahead of the style file so it gets made deliberately.
— Mauro

The render carries no prose

Three items in this order, every time. A narration block above saying what we are about to see, the bare render, a consequence block below saying what it means. Words inside the image are labels, an axis name, a row name, a number.

@maurojpelle
standalone
Keeps its furniture
A quote tweet has no voice next to it, so the headline, the takeaway band and the source line all stay on the image.
in a lane
Stripped to the render
The same asset, now sitting under a narration block that says the same words, out loud and readable.
What comes off, and where it goes
headline
into the narration block sitting directly above the render
takeaway band
into the consequence block below it, where it gets read out loud
source line
into the production note under the slot, too small to read on camera anyway
the asset
centred on the reading spine, since anything parked in the side column gets skipped
Why the render stays bare
A sentence inside a PNG can't be read from the canvas at recording size, and it duplicates the narration sitting right next to it, so you get the same words twice.
— Mauro
