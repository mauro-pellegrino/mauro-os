The 90 day build · day 1 to day 90

Everyone runs the same tool on the same default settings

Everyone in your niche opens the same model on the same defaults, so the only thing left to compete on is what you do to the output afterwards.

@maurojpelle
Everyone starts here
You run the same tool that every other person in your niche is running
Route one
You ship it on the settings it came with
What lands
It reads like the last forty posts they scrolled past
Nothing stops the scroll
Attention has nowhere to catch, so the thumb keeps moving down.
Route two
You put the effort into how it gets displayed
What lands
It sits out of place, out of order, not seen regularly
Attention actually stops
And the reader has a reason to keep going down the page.
What an obviously-AI post tells a prospect
You're a lazy fuck who doesn't care who's reading.
Slop is everywhere, so you should make an effort at making your slop look less sloppy if it makes sense, because otherwise the post answers a question they never asked out loud, which is whether the service is slop too.
— Mauro

Five phases, and each one ends with something you own

Nothing on this route finishes with "now you understand distribution", because understanding is not a thing you can open on a Monday and actually use.

@maurojpelle
Days 1 – 14
Build the brain
One folder holding everything true about your business, plus a voice doc.
Day 15
Pick one channel
The channel you are on, and the one you are deliberately not doing.
Days 15 – 45
Track the inputs
Four straight weeks of comments, replies, dms and articles, all in one sheet.
Days 30 – 60
Study five accounts
A counted study per account, and a written reason underneath every reject.
Days 45 – 90
Split by vehicle
One source shipped three ways, without reading like the same thing twice.
Why the phases compound
Every phase ends with a file, a sheet or a published piece you can point at.
— Mauro
