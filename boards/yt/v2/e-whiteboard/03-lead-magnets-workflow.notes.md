## F09 · 4:50-5:55 · Ideation is the bottleneck

The SOP itself flags ideation as the bottleneck, and it says it is worse on paper than in my head.

The triggers are a new week, a new YouTube video, an insight from a sales call, a trend, a competitor move, or a pattern from Monday tracking.

## F10 · 5:55-6:45 · Output: one topic, one line

Ideation outputs exactly two things: one topic and a one-line angle. Cadence is deliberately not fixed. A magnet ships when ideation surfaces one.

## F11 · 6:45-7:05 · The build

Part two, the build. Four phases, and one real run walked through at the end.

## F12 · 7:05-8:05 · The workflow, phase by phase

Four phases. Ideation and format. Then production, where Claude does its pass. Then packaging and distribution. Then Monday, where last week's winners get reviewed and the next ones get iterated.

## F13 · 8:05-9:00 · Pick the format from the topic

The format pick. A Notion doc with subpages, the five-pager, is the most common. Then a Gamma deck, an existing YouTube video used as the magnet, or Canva slides. The topic picks the format.

## F14 · 9:00-10:05 · The context block

Production. The context goes to Claude: topic, angle and format, plus the topic-specific inputs. The source transcript, the SOP, examples, brand context, the audience and the proof points.

The SOP flags one fix here: the skill should ask for these inputs instead of waiting to be fed them.

## F15 · 10:05-11:05 · Pick the subtype from the topic

The magnet itself has a subtype: prompt swipe file, framework, case study, YouTube video and industry-specific. The subtype comes from what the topic actually is.

The before and after for this part. Before: a framework forced into a swipe file reads as padding. A case study forced into a framework loses the one thing that made it credible, the specific account it happened in. After: the topic picks the subtype.

## F16 · 11:05-12:00 · Packaging: cover, post, DM

Packaging. A cover image. The X post copy and its DM message. The LinkedIn post copy and its LeadShark DM message. And optionally, follow-up images posted after it, each with a one-liner.

## F22 · 16:20-17:25 · One keyword per asset

That is why the keyword matters. A unique DM word per asset is the only path from a piece of content to a booked call that could be attributed.

To be straight with you: nothing in my setup joins a keyword to a booking yet. This is the design. The join is the piece that is still missing, so this part has no real worked example yet.

## F23 · 17:25-18:25 · What runs today: same-day read

What runs instead is a same-day read. A booking on day D gets credited to the posts from day D and the day before.

The script that does it calls this correlation. It never calls it attribution, and I say it the same way.

## F24 · 18:25-19:25 · Asset first, keyword second

The rule that stops this from embarrassing you. A keyword is a promise. Never publish a post that carries a keyword, or a comment-to-DM call to action, unless the resource and the thing that delivers it are already built.

Before: the post goes out and the asset is still a plan. After: the asset exists, then the post.

## F25 · 19:25-20:25 · Monday: review, iterate, exploit

Monday closes the loop. I review last week's winners, iterate on them, and exploit what worked. Then the next magnet starts from what the review found.
